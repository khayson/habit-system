H1. Day boundaries and the A26 rule for zone changes of 24 hours or more (server first, then the Dart port).
Evidence: with a single entry nothing is wrong, but across a change (a) Kiritimati -> Pago_Pago at the A26 instant makes new TimezoneTimeline([...]) throw (dates move backwards, 10 March -> 9 March), so appendChange raises InvalidArgumentException and the mutation fails with server_error forever; (b) Pago_Pago -> Kiritimati at 2026-03-10T11:00Z: the date jumps from 9 March to 11 March, but isZeroLength(10 March) is false and isZeroLength(11 March) is true (inverted), so the grid gets a phantom day; (c) Auckland -> Pago_Pago at 2026-03-10T11:00Z (exactly 24 h west): 10 March repeats but startOfLocalDay(10 March) is 11:00Z instead of 2026-03-09T11:00Z, and the previous date's end is stretched.
