# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what {{app_name}} collects about you, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it.

**The short version**

- We collect what we need to run your habit tracker and keep it in sync across your devices.
- We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
- The app has no advertising or third-party analytics tools in it.
- Your habits are private. There are no public profiles and nothing is shared with other users.
- You can export your data and delete your account.

## 1. Who is responsible

{{publisher}} ("we", "us") decides how and why your personal data is used and is responsible for it. Contact: {{contact_email}}, {{postal_address}}.

## 2. What we collect and why

| What | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| **Account details:** your name, email address, password and time zone. We store your password only as a secure one-way hash, never in a readable form. | To create your account, sign you in, send you account emails, and decide when your day starts and ends. |
| **Your habits:** names, categories, schedules, targets, start dates and reminders (a time of day and the days of the week). | To run the service. |
| **Your check-ins:** the dates and values you log, and notes if you add them. | To show your history and work out your progress. |
| **Progress we calculate:** streaks, protected days, points, level and freeze balance. | To show your progress. These come from your check-ins. |
| **Optional profile details:** a profile photo, and a "City, Country" label that you type or pick. | To personalise your profile. You can leave both empty. |
| **Device and sync details:** the name of your device, a random device identifier, the app version, your sign-in token for that device and when it was last used. | To sync your data safely and let you sign out a device. |
| **Technical logs:** IP address, request time, app version and error details. | For security, preventing abuse and fixing problems. Kept for {{log_retention_days}} days. |

Your email address is used for account and security messages (such as verifying your email and resetting your password). We do not send marketing emails.

We do not collect your precise location (GPS), your contacts, or advertising identifiers. The "City, Country" label is text you choose; the app never detects it.

## 3. Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Our servers do not send you push notifications. By default a reminder's text includes the name of the habit, so it may appear on your lock screen depending on your phone's settings. You can hide habit names in reminder text in your profile settings. You can also turn reminders off in the app, or notifications off in your phone's settings, at any time.

## 4. How we use your data, and our grounds

- **To provide the service you asked for** (our contract with you): your account, syncing, calculating progress, reminders.
- **For security and to prevent abuse** (our legitimate interests): technical logs, rate limits, sign-in protection.
- **With your consent:** your optional profile photo and location label, and notifications. You can withdraw consent by removing the photo or label, or by turning notifications off.
- **To meet legal duties** where the law requires it.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we make no automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 5. Who we share it with

Only with companies that help us run the service, under contract and only on our instructions:

- our hosting provider, {{hosting_provider}}, which stores your data on our behalf;
- a service that delivers our account emails;
- a breached-password check. When you choose a password, our server asks the Have I Been Pwned service whether it has appeared in a known data breach. Only the first five characters of a one-way hash of the password are sent. Your password and your email address are not;
- GitHub, which hosts these Terms and Privacy pages. Opening them is like visiting any website: GitHub may log your IP address and the request under its own privacy statement. We set no cookies and run no analytics on these pages.

We may also disclose data if the law or a valid legal request requires it, or to protect people's safety. If our business is ever transferred, we will tell you before your data is handled under a different policy.

## 6. Where your data lives

Our servers are with {{hosting_provider}} in {{hosting_region}}. If you live elsewhere, your data is transferred to that region to provide the service. A copy of your data is also stored on your own device, where it is protected by your phone's security. We recommend using a screen lock.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Your account and habit data:** until you delete your account.
- **After you delete your account:** your sessions end straight away; your data is removed from our live systems within 30 days; private backups age out within 90 days.
- **Records of deletions** (so your other devices learn that something was deleted): kept for up to 180 days.
- **Export files:** the download link works for 24 hours.
- **Profile photo:** deleted when you replace or remove it, or delete your account. The original upload is discarded once the app's sizes are made, and location information inside the photo is removed.
- **Technical logs:** {{log_retention_days}} days.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **See and export your data** from the app.
- **Correct it** by editing it in the app.
- **Delete it** by deleting your account, or by removing a photo, label or habit.
- **Ask us** to restrict or stop certain uses, or to give you your data in a portable format, by emailing {{contact_email}}.

Depending on where you live, including under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and, where it applies, the EU or UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and move your data, and to withdraw consent. We will answer within the time the law requires. If you are not happy with how we handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ghana or to the data protection authority where you live.

## 9. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Passwords are stored hashed. A sign-in token is kept for each device and can be revoked; signing out or deleting your account revokes it. Profile photos are checked and re-encoded, kept in private storage and never given a public address. No system is perfectly secure. If you find a weakness, please email {{contact_email}}. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

## 10. Children

The service is not for anyone under {{minimum_age}}. If we learn that we hold data from someone below that age, we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change matters to you, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
